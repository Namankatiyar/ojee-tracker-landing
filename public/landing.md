## 2. Interactive Workspace Cockpit (Core Feature Modules)

### Module 1: Subtopic Syllabus Planner (JEE & NEET)
- **Role**: Replaces shallow chapter checklists with deep subtopic-level verification.
- **Subjects Covered**:
  - *Physics*: Mechanics, Electrodynamics, Optics, Thermodynamics, Modern Physics.
  - *Chemistry*: Physical Chemistry, Inorganic Chemistry, Organic Chemistry (GOC, Reaction Mechanisms).
  - *Mathematics*: Calculus, Coordinate Geometry, Algebra, Vectors & 3D, Trigonometry.
  - *Biology (NEET)*: Botany, Zoology, Genetics, Human Physiology, Ecology.
- **4 Milestone Checkpoints per Subtopic**:
  1. **NCERT**: In-depth textbook reading and NCERT back-exercise completion.
  2. **PYQs**: Previous Year Questions solved and documented.
  3. **Modules**: Advanced coaching exercises and question banks completed.
  4. **Mock Tests**: Topic-level and chapter-level speed tests attempted.

### Module 2: JEE & NEET Time Tracker
- **Role**: Precision deep-work timer integrated with subject tracking.
- **Timer Modes**:
  - **Pomodoro Mode**: 25-minute focus sprints with automated 5-minute recovery intervals.
  - **Stopwatch Mode**: Continuous elapsed-time tracking for uninterrupted mock test simulations.
  - **Custom Intervals**: 45-minute and 60-minute subject-block timers.
- **Completely Ad-Free**: Your concentration is never broken by advertisements or sponsored banners.

### Module 3: Daily Prep Planner & Weekly Study Tasks
- **Role**: High-speed task management backlog organized by subject tags.
- **Capabilities**:
  - Fast task entry (`> Add task to weekly planner...`).
  - Subject tags (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics) with distinct visual contrast.
  - Quick checkbox completion with strike-through and local persistence.

### Module 4: JEE & NEET Mock Score Tracker
- **Role**: Test series performance ledger and accuracy tracker.
- **Metrics Logged**:
  - Mock Examination Title (e.g. *JEE Advanced Mock 8*, *JEE Main Mock 14*, *NEET Full Mock 4*).
  - Score obtained vs Maximum marks (e.g. 198 / 300 or 650 / 720).
  - Date of completion.
  - Subject-level accuracy trajectory.

### Module 5: BLUE AI Study Planner
- **Role**: Diagnostic assistant that evaluates test score logs and syllabus completion velocity to recommend prioritized revision blocks.

### Module 6: Time Tracker & Prep Analytics Reports
- **Role**: Visual and tabular analytics summarizing deep work hours.
- **Metrics Provided**:
  - Weekly Total: e.g. **62.4 Hours** (+12% improvement vs previous week).
  - Subject Distribution:
    - Physics: 28.4 Hours
    - Chemistry: 20.0 Hours
    - Mathematics: 14.0 Hours
  - Daily Dataset: Mon (8.5h), Tue (9.2h), Wed (6.0h), Thu (7.8h), Fri (10.5h), Sat (12.0h), Sun (8.4h).

### Module 7: Peer Accountability Network
- **Role**: Distraction-free accountability feed linking study peers.
- **Capabilities**:
  - Live status indicators (e.g., *"Aman Rathore: Solving Matrices PYQs — 7.8 hrs today"*).
  - Friendly daily hour comparisons without social media doom-scrolling.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: Is OJEE-Tracker really 100% free and completely ad-free?
**Answer**: Yes! OJEE-Tracker offers a 100% completely ad-free experience forever. There are zero banner ads, video popups, sponsored coaching promotions, or user tracking cookies. Every core tool—including the subtopic syllabus planner, study time tracker, mock score tracker, and peer accountability network—is unrestricted and completely free at ₹0.

### Q2: How does OJEE-Tracker work as a preparation and prep tracker for JEE and NEET students?
**Answer**: OJEE-Tracker is an all-in-one preparation tracker that combines four vital study systems: (1) a granular syllabus planner that breaks chapters into subtopics, (2) an integrated deep work time tracker with Pomodoro and stopwatch modes, (3) a mock score tracker that records test series trends, and (4) a live peer accountability network. Everything runs offline-first with zero lag.

### Q3: How does the syllabus planner organize JEE and NEET topics down to subtopics?
**Answer**: Unlike generic checklist apps that only list broad chapter names, OJEE-Tracker breaks each chapter down into specific conceptual subtopics (e.g., in Rotational Dynamics: Moment of Inertia, Angular Momentum, Pure Rolling). For every subtopic, you can independently tick off 4 key milestones: NCERT theory reading, Previous Year Questions (PYQs), coaching modules, and chapter mock tests.

### Q4: How does the study time tracker help JEE and NEET students maintain focus?
**Answer**: The built-in time tracker features both 25-minute Pomodoro cycles and a continuous deep work stopwatch. Sessions are tagged by subject (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology) and automatically aggregated into daily study hours reports. Because OJEE-Tracker is completely ad-free, your focus is never interrupted by intrusive ads or popups.

### Q5: How does the mock score tracker record test performance for JEE and NEET?
**Answer**: The mock score tracker acts as a dedicated test series ledger. You can log test titles, dates, and scores out of 300 marks (JEE Main), 360 marks (JEE Advanced), or 720 marks (NEET). It helps you visualize accuracy trends, identify weak subjects, and track your trajectory toward your target cutoff percentile.

### Q6: Does OJEE-Tracker work offline without an active internet connection?
**Answer**: Yes! OJEE-Tracker is architected offline-first. All your syllabus ticks, study time tracker logs, revision stages, and mock test scores are saved locally in your browser storage (IndexedDB / LocalStorage). You can continue studying and tracking smoothly during internet outages or in airplane mode.
