## What Is Included at ₹0

### 1. Syllabus Planner & Prep Tracker (JEE & NEET)
- **Granular Subtopic Breakdown**: Complete official syllabi for JEE Main, JEE Advanced, and NEET UG broken down to conceptual subtopics.
- **Four Verification Milestones**: Track NCERT readings, PYQ problem solving, coaching modules, and mock tests for every chapter.
- **Offline-First Storage**: All syllabus states, revision cycles, and logs are stored locally in the browser (IndexedDB).
- **Progressive Web App (PWA)**: Installable standalone app on Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, and Linux.

### 2. Time Tracker & Deep Work Study Clock
- **Pomodoro & Stopwatch Modes**: Built-in 25-minute Pomodoro timer and continuous deep-work stopwatch with subject tagging.
- **Friends Network Live Time**: View live peer study statuses (e.g., *"Aman Rathore: Solving Matrices PYQs — 7.8 hrs today"*).
- **Study Hour Comparisons**: Compare daily and weekly deep work hours with study partners.
- **Visual Analytics**: Daily time tracker charts breaking down study hours across Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.

### 3. Mock Score Tracker & Completely Ad-Free Experience
- **Dedicated Score Ledger**: Track test series scores out of 300 (JEE) or 720 (NEET), dates, and score trajectories.
- **Completely Ad-Free**: Zero advertisements, zero affiliate banners, and zero promotional interruptions during study sessions.
- **Absolute Privacy**: Your study data never leaves your device unless you explicitly opt in to peer metric sharing.
- **100% Open Source**: Freedom to inspect code, contribute custom state syllabi, or self-host under GNU GPLv3.

## Why Is OJEE-Tracker Completely Ad-Free and Free?

As students who went through the grueling competitive exam grind, we were frustrated with expensive coaching paywalls and ad-bloated apps that constantly distract students with course promotions and popups.

We believe that disciplined study tracking, syllabus transparency, and focused time tracking should be universally accessible without distraction. OJEE-Tracker is built as an ad-free public good for every serious student.
